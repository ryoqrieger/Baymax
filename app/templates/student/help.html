Frequently asked

How does a request work?+

Pick a professional and press “Request appointment”. They see your request and either accept it with a time or decline it. You will see the reply on your dashboard.

Who can see my answers?+

Your answers are private to you and the professionals involved in your care. University authorities only see combined statistics, never individual answers.

What do the status levels mean?+

Stable means low distress. Challenged means a noticeable level of stress, worry or low mood, so extra support may help. Critical means a high level, and talking to a professional is recommended.

How often can I take the assessment?+

Once per semester. A new one opens when the next semester starts.
